Research and learning for better decisions.

I work with hospitality organizations, education providers, and research partners to understand consumer behavior and design learning programs. Together, we turn a question or a learning need into research, useful materials, and a plan for delivery.

Research & learning consultation

Where research
can help.

A learning program that needs a clear structure. A guest survey that raises new questions. A technology pilot whose value is still unclear. Research helps define what to examine, teach, and evaluate.

Work can begin with a focused evidence review or develop into a custom study, ongoing advisory support, or a learning program.

Learning program design

From needs assessment and curriculum design to slides, cases, activities, online modules, delivery, and evaluation. University courses, micro-credentials, executive education, and professional workshops are shaped around what learners need to do.

Possible outputs: a needs assessment, a program roadmap, a complete course, teaching materials, or a workshop with a learning evaluation plan.

Study design & analysis

Guest and employee surveys, experiments, concept tests, analysis of existing data and reviews, and before-and-after evaluation.

Possible outputs: a research plan, an analyzed dataset, a findings report, or a measurement framework.

Employee engagement survey partnerships are delivered in collaboration with Dr. Tia Mistry.

Environment & experience research

Research into design, atmosphere, sound, sensory cues, and biophilic elements; guest journey research; and concept evaluation alongside designers and operators. The focus is how people perceive, feel, and respond within a service experience.

Possible outputs: an experience audit, a design research brief, a concept study, or a plan to measure changes in guest response.

Technology & AI evaluation

Vendor-neutral research on guest acceptance, accessibility, trust, and the handoff between technology and people.

Possible outputs: an evidence review, an evaluation framework, or a technology pilot study.

Working together

From question
to useful evidence.

The research question or learning need, scope, and outputs are agreed around the decision or capability you want to develop.

  1. Frame the question.

    Discuss the situation, the people it affects, the evidence you already have, and what a useful answer would change.

  2. Choose the right approach.

    Define the study, curriculum, evidence review, or evaluation. Separate what published research shows from what still needs to be tested in your context.

  3. Make the findings usable.

    Bring the evidence together in a report, research brief, measurement plan, or learning program that supports your next step.
