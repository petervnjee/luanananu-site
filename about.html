University faculty profile ↗

Luana Nanu is a consumer behavior researcher and educator, an assistant professor at the University of South Florida, and coordinator of the M3 Center for Hospitality Technology and Innovation.

Her research examines how people perceive, feel, and make decisions in service settings. Biophilic design and other environmental cues, technology and AI, and media and storytelling are complementary areas of her work, with hospitality providing a context for understanding consumer behavior.

At USF, she teaches hospitality operations, technology, analytics, and lodging management. Her education work extends from course design and content creation to program development, online delivery, and evaluation. She builds learning experiences for university students and industry professionals, connecting research with practice.
